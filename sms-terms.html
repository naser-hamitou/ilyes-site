SMS, Terms & Privacy

Messaging and policy information.

This page contains the SMS messaging terms, Terms & Conditions, and Privacy Policy for the dental practice of Dr. Ilyes Amrani in Montreal, Canada. It explains how phone numbers are collected online, how SMS consent is obtained, and how to opt out at any time.

SMS Terms

Text updates for patient care.

Campaign name: Dr. Ilyes Amrani phone campaign. SMS messages are used for appointment scheduling, reminders, follow-up coordination, and direct responses to patient or prospective patient inquiries.

How consent is collected (Call-to-Action)

Phone numbers are collected online only, through the Contact Us form on our website at drilyesamrani.netlify.app. To opt in, a visitor voluntarily enters their phone number into that web form, checks the SMS-consent checkbox (which is never pre-checked), and submits the form. Providing a phone number and SMS consent is optional and is never a condition of any purchase or service. We do not collect phone numbers for SMS by any other method.

Disclaimer shown on the form, directly above the submit button: “By entering a telephone number into this online form, checking the box above, and submitting it on this website, you are consenting to be contacted by SMS text message and agreeing to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of further messaging. Reply HELP for support contact.”

Who Sends

Dr. Ilyes Amrani and authorized clinic staff may send messages related to dental care coordination.

Who Receives

Patients and prospective patients who voluntarily provide a phone number online through the Contact Us form on this website may receive messages.

Campaign Type

Customer care and appointment reminders for dental services. The campaign is not used for lending, loans, financing offers, or age-gated content.

Content

  • Appointment requests and scheduling updates
  • Appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Care coordination and inquiry responses
  • No age-gated content, lending or loan offers, unrelated third-party promotions, or marketing messages from affiliates

Frequency

Message frequency varies by request and appointment activity, typically up to 4 messages per appointment or inquiry.

Consent

Providing a phone number is optional. Phone numbers are collected online only through the Contact Us form on this website. If you enter one into that form, check the SMS-consent checkbox, and submit it, you consent to receive SMS messages about your request. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Opt Out & Help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messaging. Reply HELP to receive an automated response containing our support contact: email Drilyesamrani@gmail.com, phone +1 (438) 320-7431, and support website drilyesamrani.netlify.app. Message and data rates may apply.

Links

Messages may include links only when relevant to appointment scheduling, patient forms, clinic directions, or practice information.

Sample messages reviewed for this campaign:

Dr. Ilyes Amrani: We received your appointment request and will follow up with available times. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Dr. Ilyes Amrani: Your dental appointment is scheduled for [date] at [time]. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

HELP auto-reply (sent when a recipient texts HELP):
